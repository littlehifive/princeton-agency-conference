The flagship study: measure agency where it already shows

Research · with Hamsa Bastani and Osbert Bastani, University of Pennsylvania · the team behind the PNAS 2025 guardrails study

Why naturalistic data

Digital platforms already produce the evidence: conversations, decisions, and, where partners can link them, real outcomes.

Surveys, today's default, interrupt people, carry self-report bias, and sample thin snapshots in time.

Traces are continuous: agency can be observed as it develops, unobtrusively, over months instead of moments.

How the method works

1

Transcripts

Text, voice, and video from real platform use.

2

LLM judge

Reads them against a structured rubric grounded in Social Cognitive Theory: intentionality, forethought, self-reactiveness, self-reflectiveness.

3

Calibrated scores

Agency scores that carry uncertainty and say so when the evidence is missing.

4

Validation

Expert labels and lightweight micro-surveys check the judge where it matters most.

The goal is a shared, validated instrument for reading agency in the wild, built and tested with six NGO partners.
