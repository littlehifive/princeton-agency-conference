NE

Nova Escola

Brazil

Product

A WhatsApp lesson planner built on 40 years of teacher content and Brazil's national curriculum, made for low-connectivity classrooms.

Data

150,000 teachers generated half a million lesson plans in the first year, and 76,127 in February 2026 alone.

Study

Does co-creating plans in conversation build lasting teaching practice, or only save time? Vary copilot features and measure what teachers learn.

TAP

The Apprentice Project

India

Product

TAP Buddy guides students ages 9 to 17 through hands-on projects in coding, art, science, and financial literacy over WhatsApp; a new voice AI reaches parents and students in Hindi, Marathi, and Punjabi.

Data

Multimodal project submissions (text, images, video) plus the new voice transcripts from family calls.

Study

Measure creativity and 21st-century skills as agency signals, and learn how bot-delivered feedback changes what students attempt next.

U

Udhyam Learning Foundation

India

Product

Students form teams, receive seed money, run real businesses, and pitch on video; an AI help bot answers program and learning questions around the clock.

Data

Help-bot chats, idea submissions, pitch videos, and sales records, with outcome data on about 2,000 students, plus a McGill-led classroom observation study.

Study

Infer grit, motivation, and self-efficacy from chats and submissions, and feed the findings back into both the in-person program and the AI buddy.
