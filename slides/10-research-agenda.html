What the lab studies · Research projects

Four questions set the research agenda

1

Does agency-centric AI outperform directive AI?

When an assistant is explicitly designed to build goal-setting and navigation, do users gain more agency than with a purely informational tool?

2

Which conversational strategies strengthen agency?

Which micro-interventions and theory-based techniques, embedded in AI conversations, move agency the most?

3

How should humans and AI share the work?

How do hybrid programs keep AI synergizing with human support, rather than replacing it, across contexts and populations?

4

Can agency be measured from conversations?

Can it be inferred reliably from human-AI transcripts, and do its linguistic markers differ across cultures, languages, and domains?

Now running · Noora Health

Noora Health's programs reach 2 million users in 11 languages, with about 10,000 incoming messages a day. Since late 2025, an AI co-pilot has helped staff answer families while humans keep control of every reply.

We are studying whether the co-pilot changes how staff write, and how families respond, across Bangladesh, India, and Nepal.
