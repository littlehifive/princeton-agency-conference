Designing AI-personalized coaching to support field-agent agency in household behavior change

A design and evaluation study with Raising The Village's SBCC AI Coach

The setting

Raising The Village's SBCC AI Coach generates personalized behavior-change coaching messages for last-mile households. Field agents review and deliver the messages across Uganda, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of Congo, so the AI intervention runs through human judgment rather than replacing it.

The study · personalization and adoption

Design

Can AI-generated coaching make SBCC messages more contextual, actionable, and culturally relevant while preserving field agents' discretion?

Agency

Do agents experience the system as a tool that expands their coaching capacity, or as a script that narrows their professional judgment?

Measure

Track practice adoption, coaching coverage, field-agent knowledge, and household outcomes as the rollout moves from message-quality validation into impact evaluation.

Next

A cluster-randomized personalization trial is planned for the 2026B-2027 cycle against household outcomes.

The design question is whether AI can help field agents deliver better behavior-change support while strengthening, rather than bypassing, the human relationships that make coaching work.
