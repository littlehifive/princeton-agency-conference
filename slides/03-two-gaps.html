Working with these organizations surfaced two gaps related to human agency in AI product work

Observation from the accelerator

AI tools are advancing service delivery faster than the field is learning what they do to people’s agency.

The Design Gap

AI products are built to optimize service delivery, not explicitly to strengthen people’s agency.

Despite AI's potential to strengthen human agency, NGO products often treat agency as a byproduct rather than a design objective.

Decades of behavioral science research identify agency as a key mechanism for improving development outcomes, yet it is rarely built into AI design, testing, or iteration.

The Measurement Gap

AI products are underused as windows into how agency evolves.

Many products may not generate as many signals of agency as tools like ChatGPT or Claude Code do. When signals exist, they often come from power users, and the field lacks a shared standard or analytical toolsfor finding, gathering, and interpreting agency inside human-AI traces.
