How the measurement works

From raw transcripts to trusted scores

Step 1

Transcripts

Text, voice, and video from real platform use, plus linked real-world decisions where partners have them.

Step 2

LLM judge

Fills a structured rubric for each user and time window, guided by in-context examples tailored to the domain.

Step 3

Calibrated scores

A learned model turns rubric answers into agency scores that carry confidence estimates.

Step 4

Validation

Multiple experts label the same transcripts, and lightweight micro-surveys ask users directly at the most informative moments.

What makes this hard, and how we handle it

Two kinds of agency

Toward end goals, and toward the platform itself. The rubric covers both.

Agency moves over time

Scores decompose into an overall and a time-varying component.

Missing evidence

When a transcript cannot support a judgment, the score says so.

User burden

Micro-surveys run under a strict per-user budget, placed where they are worth the interruption.
