We put the method in a room

Field-tested · Nairobi onsite · July 2026

1

Teams of grantees, funders, and staff coded 30 synthetic AI-assistant conversations from education, health, and agriculture.

2

Everyone labeled the same binary rubric, built on the agency flywheel: motivation, action and choice, links to real-world outcomes, and updated mental models.

3

Calibrate showed people-vs-people agreement live. Then an LLM judge ran the exact same rubric on the same conversations.

What the room learned

Binary questions force choices on borderline cases, and those forced choices surface the definitional debates that actually matter for measurement.

Once a rubric earns agreement between people, an AI can apply it consistently across thousands of conversations. That is what makes agency measurement scale.
