The operating model

Every project runs on a tri-sector team

TAF

The Agency Fund, the backbone

  • Manages NGO relationships and executes the data, legal, and ethics agreements.
  • Maintains shared data and experimentation infrastructure, so each new study starts fast and runs with minimal engineering overhead.
R

Researchers, academic and industry

  • Lead time-bound field studies, typically 3 to 6 months, co-designed with TAF and the NGO.
  • Leave at least one reusable artifact behind: a dataset, a measurement scale, a model, or an intervention script. Methods ship openly unless ethics or law restrict them.
NGO

NGO partners, the ground truth

  • Keep projects anchored in frontline reality: recruitment, implementation, and the interpretation of findings.
  • Co-own learning goals, and retain ownership of their data and their users' data.

Every study is time-boxed and leaves a reusable artifact behind, and every method ships open by default.
