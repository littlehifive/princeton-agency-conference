Evaluating AI Across Cultures: A Multilevel Framework for Generative AI in Global Development

Culture is not a variable to control for. It operates as a set of defaults — invisible assumptions about what "good" looks like — baked into every evaluation tool.

A working group across

The Agency Fund · Stanford · Microsoft Research · Center for Global Development · Carnegie Mellon · Michigan State · UT Dallas · George Washington

Level 1Model

Benchmarks test Western academic knowledge in English — and say nothing about care advice in the Rift Valley.

Level 2Product

Engagement metrics inherit commercial defaults: attention is not utility, and more usage is not more welfare.

Level 3User

Psychological measures assume WEIRD constructs and survey norms that do not travel across cultures.

Level 4Impact

Trials chase a moving target: model updates and prompt changes silently shift the product mid-study.

When the tool's defaults don't match the deployment context, evaluation produces artifacts, not evidence.
