Six partners, six ways to read agency from real products

Research · what we might learn with each organization · health, education, and livelihoods across five countries

JH

Jacaranda Health

Health · Kenya

PROMPTS, maternal-health SMS with AI triage; 3.8M+ mothers reached.

Read how mothers navigate care pathways, link those signals to actual care visits, and design proactive nudges for mothers who do not yet know what to ask.

DG

Digital Green

Livelihoods · Kenya · Ethiopia · India

FarmerChat agronomy advisor; 1.3M farmers.

Find agency signals among power users, track how they shift from version 1 to version 2, and test whether they predict real-world adoption.

KA

Kabakoo Academies

Livelihoods · Mali

WhatsApp AI mentor with video reflections, often in Bambara.

Mine reflections for agency signals and connect them to income and community standing in the years ahead, under strict privacy protections.

NE

Nova Escola

Education · Brazil

WhatsApp lesson planner on the national curriculum; 150K teachers.

Test whether co-creating plans in conversation builds lasting teaching practice or only saves time, by varying copilot features.

TAP

The Apprentice Project

Education · India

TAP Buddy project-based learning, plus mother-tongue voice AI.

Measure creativity and 21st-century skills as agency signals, and learn how AI feedback changes what students attempt next.

U

Udhyam Learning Foundation

Education · India

AI help bot inside a real-business entrepreneurship program.

Infer grit, motivation, and self-efficacy from chats and submissions, and feed the findings back into the program and the bot.

Every partner runs a live AI product with real users and real stakes. Together they give the study conversations in text, voice, and video across languages and cultures.
