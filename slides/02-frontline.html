AI has already reached the frontline

Through the AI for Global Development Accelerator, run with OpenAI and the Center for Global Development, we watched dozens of NGOs build and deploy AI for people living in difficult circumstances.

JH

Jacaranda Health

Kenya

PROMPTS

Two-way SMS guidance for new and expecting mothers: answers, risk triage, and referrals into care. More than 3.8 million mothers reached.

DG

Digital Green

Kenya, Ethiopia, India

FarmerChat

An AI advisor that gives smallholder farmers tailored agronomy advice in their own language. 1.3 million farmers on the platform.

RL

Rocket Learning

India

WhatsApp coaching

Personalized coaching that helps community daycare workers run better early childhood education, delivered where they already are.

These tools deliver real value, and they give us a window we have never had into the daily lives of mothers, farmers, and teachers. The open question is what they do to the people who use them.
