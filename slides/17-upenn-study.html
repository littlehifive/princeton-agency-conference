Digital platforms already produce conversations, decisions, and, where partners can link them, real outcomes. We design LLM judges that read those transcripts against a structured rubric and infer each component of agency, with scores that admit uncertainty.

Intentionality

Does the user form plans that serve their goals?

Forethought

Do they weigh what would happen under different courses of action?

Self-reactiveness

Do they carry plans out and adapt when new information arrives?

Self-reflectiveness

Do they look back on outcomes to improve the next decision?

Framework grounded in Social Cognitive Theory; it extends to other definitions of agency, including the capability approach.
