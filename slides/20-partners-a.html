The cohort in detail · 1 of 2

What each partner brings, and what we will learn together

DG

Digital Green

Kenya · Ethiopia · India

Product

FarmerChat answers smallholders' agronomy questions; version 2 adds visual learning cards and profile-based personalization.

Data

1.3 million users and hundreds of thousands of conversations, linkable to adoption and yield surveys from ongoing RCTs.

Study

Find agency signals among power users, track how they shift from version 1 to version 2, and test whether they predict real adoption, alongside the World Bank MAGNET agency measures Digital Green already uses.

JH

Jacaranda Health

Kenya

Product

PROMPTS supports new and expecting mothers over two-way SMS with answers, AI risk triage, and referrals into care.

Data

Conversation logs from a service that has reached 3.8M+ mothers, linkable to routine surveys and care-visit records.

Study

Read how mothers navigate care pathways, link those signals to visits, and design proactive nudges for mothers who do not yet know what to ask.

KA

Kabakoo Academies

Mali

Product

A WhatsApp AI mentor guides youth upskilling; learners answer curriculum prompts with short reflection videos, often in Bambara.

Data

About 5,000 learners, 15,000 reflections, and 4,000 video transcripts. A waitlist RCT already showed gains of 0.14 to 0.25 SD in growth mindset.

Study

Mine reflections for agency signals and connect them to income and community standing years later, under strict privacy protections for at-risk youth.
