What the Accelerator taught us

Two gaps sit between these tools and lasting impact

The Design Gap

AI products are built to optimize service delivery, not to strengthen agency.

Decades of research show how psychologically wise interventions shift meaning, unlock agency, and improve development outcomes. That knowledge rarely reaches AI product design, testing, or iteration.

The Measurement Gap

AI products are underused as windows into how agency evolves.

For AI companies, engagement is a fine proxy for revenue. In the social sector, usage alone does not produce learning, health, or income, and it can even work against them. We analyzed 395,717 conversations on one partner platform: the average lasted 3.1 turns. The signals are there, and almost nobody is measuring them.
