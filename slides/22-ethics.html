Ethics and data stewardship

The data belongs to the people it describes

Data are owned by participating NGOs and the communities and individuals they serve. Research access is de-identified, tiered, logged, and time-bound.

Data minimization

Collect only what the approved research purpose needs.

Informed consent

Culturally appropriate consent for every participant.

Purpose limitation

No partner may repurpose data beyond the agreed scope.

Ethical review

Every project goes through ethical review and follows responsible-AI practice.

Open by default

Tools, models, and methods ship open-source, with contributors credited.

Honest narratives

No extractive, misleading, or decontextualized storytelling about the people behind the data.
