What A²L is

One lab with two jobs

The Agency & AI Lab (A²L) is a decentralized, virtual, and open research and learning lab. We define agency as a person's capability to navigate toward the future they want.

01

Design AI for human agency

Bring the science of wise interventions into AI products through prompting strategies, advisory knowledge bases, and evaluation frameworks, so tools support people to think, choose, and act with confidence.

For NGO builders and the researchers who advise them

02

Measure how agency evolves with AI

Track internally experienced agency, what people come to believe about themselves and the AI, and externally expressed agency, the goals people set and pursue directly, through systems, and together with others.

From surveys, log data, and the conversations themselves

Every A²L project serves both jobs: better designs create the data that better measures need, and better measures make better designs possible.
